# Search Owl

Isolated public deployment of Search Owl. This repository intentionally contains only the web page, bounded search service, real CC0 barn-owl image and its license record.

Optional provider credentials are read only from server environment variables. None are committed. Google Translate web handoffs work without credentials; in-page Cloud Translation remains unavailable until a separately approved key and billing account exist.

Automatic no-key coverage is bounded to official/public metadata interfaces: Wikipedia, Library of Congress, Open Library, Crossref, Europe PMC, World Bank Documents & Reports, Project Gutenberg, Internet Archive, and DuckDuckGo Instant Answers. General web engines, national collections without a supported API, and official exchange/regulator sites are query-preserving browser handoffs and are never counted as collected results.

Search responses are fetched on demand and cached for at most two minutes. Provider publication/indexing delays still apply. Search Owl does not claim real-time securities quotes; licensed feeds such as NSE real-time and delayed market products are intentionally not redistributed.

Browser integration is available through `/opensearch.xml`. Browsers without OpenSearch discovery can use `https://search-owl.onrender.com/?q=%s` as a custom site-search URL.

The homepage rotates through 235 verified owl species and 235 distinct Wikimedia Commons photographs. The automatic selection advances at midnight in `America/New_York`; visitors can freely choose any catalog owl for the current New York day. Every photograph displays its creator, license, and Commons source, and the original local Barn Owl remains the offline fallback. Regenerate the catalog with `node scripts/build-owl-catalog.mjs`.

The page adds rule-based context panels for mathematics, space, places, government, economics and local-service searches. Optional location details are used only when the visitor supplies them for a location-relevant query. Short follow-up continuity stays in that browser tab through session storage; Search Owl does not create a server-side user profile.

Decorative city, gaming, fashion, automotive and fuel subjects can use a small, attributed Wikimedia Commons collage around the page edges. The shared image pipeline accepts raster images only and requires a distinguishing query term to appear in the file title; irrelevant document covers, loose generic matches, and metadata-only coincidences are rejected. Each picture is explicitly labeled as context only, links to its source, and displays the listed creator, license, title-match basis and source date when available. A title match is not presented as independent proof of a camera location, capture date, current appearance, facility identity or retrieved claim. Current-event searches deliberately keep generic decoration so pictures cannot be mistaken for evidence of an incident. Explicit decorative imagery is suppressed; this does not filter or alter legitimate search results. When a safely attributed picture is unavailable, generated CSS scenes provide a neutral fallback. The centered owl and results below it never move. On wide screens the attributed collage uses the page edges; at medium and narrow widths it becomes an inline gallery so contextual imagery remains visible without covering results. A directory shortcut row near the search box makes automotive, securities-exchange, and agricultural discovery reachable without scanning the full page.

Fuel-price searches keep retail station prices distinct from crude-oil or securities data. Optional location details may narrow the provider handoffs, but Search Owl never invents a station price or calls a price live without a supported feed and displayed timestamp.

Real-estate searches are location-aware and use attributed architecture imagery with a warning that decoration does not depict or verify a listing. Sixteen regional portal handoffs cover representative markets across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, Africa and South America. Search Owl does not scrape or aggregate their inventory and never treats an asking price or advertised rent as a completed transaction price.

Hospital discovery uses thirteen government, public health-system and national facility-directory handoffs across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, the Middle East and South America. It distinguishes hospitals from clinics and other services, does not claim exhaustive world coverage, and does not rank clinical quality, diagnose conditions or invent facility details. General amenity searches use six reputable map/local-directory handoffs for user-supplied localities; Search Owl does not claim a complete point-of-interest database or invent hours, prices, availability or services.

Financial discovery includes a searchable, region-organized directory of 48 major official securities exchanges and exchange groups across North America, South America, Europe, Africa, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, verified October 21, 2026. It explicitly covers ASX in Sydney, B3 in São Paulo, Bolsa de Valores de Caracas, and the Santiago/Lima/Colombia exchanges within the nuam regional holding. The directory is broad rather than falsely described as exhaustive and links to the World Federation of Exchanges membership directory for wider authoritative discovery. Search Owl does not republish exchange quote streams. Displayed handoff notes preserve documented delay or licensing boundaries and distinguish Beijing BSE from Bombay BSE.

The Nasdaq Nordic handoff carries a specific visual warning: Nasdaq's external European Market Activity “Latest Listings” cards reuse Nasdaq tower/listing-event photography. City labels such as Helsinki and Stockholm describe the listing market, not the photographed skyline or building location. Search Owl does not control or relabel those external images.

Agricultural discovery is deliberately separate from securities markets. Nine official, no-cost source handoffs cover global FAO monitoring plus Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam, Laos, the United States, and the European Union. Labels distinguish farm-gate, purchase-point, wholesale, retail, bulletin, and dashboard data. A cited Thailand OAE example preserves the exact Hom Mali 105 paddy-rice observations published over the prior 14 days, including item/grade, unit, currency, locality, market type, observation dates, and retrieval date; missing dates are not invented or interpolated. The page does not claim that local markets form one universal market or that every farmers market is covered.

The agriculture section also provides a shared worldwide location hub. Visitors choose a continent or world region, country or territory, category, and then an administrative area or locality. Categories cover local markets, farmers markets, public and produce markets, bazaars, price sources, libraries and archives, official weather warnings, hospitals, stock exchanges, real estate, and nearby places. Exchange selection filters Search Owl's verified venue directory by country; the other non-market categories combine a location-specific OpenStreetMap search with Search Owl's established category directory. The United States market category has a state-and-locality lookup backed by a dated October 22, 2026 snapshot of the USDA AMS Local Food Directories keyless bulk feed, covering all 50 states plus the District of Columbia and returning actual self-reported market records. Canada and Mexico include complete province/state choices, including Quebec; other countries accept the locally appropriate province, region, district, county, or city name. Outside the United States, the market category opens a location-specific OpenStreetMap discovery search for local-market terms and routes visitors to an authoritative price or directory source such as Canada market information, Mexico SNIIM, Thailand DIT, Vietnam IPSARD, FAO/GIEWS for Laos and other countries, or the EU Agri-food Data Portal. Mapped locations and hours remain distinct from dated commodity and retail price reports, and incomplete voluntary or map coverage is disclosed.

The library and archive directory uses the same region-to-country architecture for North America, Central America and the Caribbean, South America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia, Oceania (including Australia), and dedicated Antarctica/Arctic research groupings that are not misrepresented as sovereign countries. It contains curated national institutions and public digital portals across Europe and representative sources worldwide. Every country remains selectable; an uncurated country receives visibly labeled IFLA, ICA, and Open Library fallbacks rather than invented national links. Unicode query text is passed only through verified search templates. Official weather-warning handoffs use selected national agencies where curated, Meteoalarm for supported European coverage, or the WMO Severe Weather Information Centre; Search Owl does not infer no-alert status, issue/expiry times, or worldwide real-time coverage from those links.

The daily owl horoscope is an optional, deterministic entertainment feature. It asks only for a zodiac-sign choice, stores no birth date or name, identifies the displayed date and original Search Owl source, and explicitly disclaims scientific, professional-advice, and outcome-prediction authority.

Exact-place imagery is handled conservatively. USDA records link to an exact-address map search that can show contributed place photos when available; the worldwide hub does the same for its selected locality and category. Search Owl does not have a reliable global official facility-photo feed and never labels its generic topic decoration as a photograph of a named market or hospital. A map photo is an identification aid, not proof of legitimacy, quality, services, hours, or current operation.

Automotive searches receive a dedicated contextual theme and related-search suggestions for official models, versions/trims, model years, configurators, list prices, specifications, recalls, and safety ratings. Thirteen official manufacturer handoffs cover representative major markets including the United States, United Kingdom, Germany/Europe, France, Spain, Poland, Japan, South Korea, India, China, Australia, and Brazil. A separate Carsales Australia marketplace handoff adds Australian new/used listings while explicitly labeling advertised asking prices as different from verified transaction prices. Search Owl requires the visitor to compare country, model year, body style, powertrain, trim, local currency, taxes/fees, listing or observation date, and price type; it does not claim a universal vehicle-price feed.
